The studio on your server

What runs here

fenecdb itself, compiled to WebAssembly (243 KB gzipped) and run on a worker of this tab: the engine fenec-server runs, answering the studio as a server would. It holds three collections, made as the page opens:

  • products, searched by the words of their description (@text) and by meaning, with a vector of eight (@hnsw);
  • orders, which name a product and come back beside it with lookup;
  • a week of a shop's events, counted by the hour with bucket, having and approx_count_distinct.

Write whatever you like, create collection and create index too: Reset data puts every collection back. The database lives in this tab's memory and goes with it, unless you keep it in this browser. The language is in the FenecQL reference.

On your server

The same studio, served by the server to its operators, over its own data and with its token:

fenec-server --file data.fenec \
  --http-token "$TOKEN" --studio
open http://127.0.0.1:8080/_studio/

There it signs in, holds a scoped token to the rows its policy allows, and adds the server's own numbers. How the studio works.
